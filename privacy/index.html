Rail Parade — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026
Publisher: J.R. Capital Group Pty Ltd
Contact: jr.dev.space@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the Rail Parade game for Android uses, and why.

Summary

  • There is no Rail Parade account, and we run no servers that receive your data.
  • Your progress is stored on your device. If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is also saved to your Google account.
  • Google Play handles payments. Google's advertising service shows ads, according to your privacy choices.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services, request your precise location, or offer player chat.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your puzzle progress, settings and purchase records on your device so it can keep your progress and deliver items you buy. It also keeps a small diagnostic log of app and device details and errors. That log stays on your device and is never uploaded automatically.

Cloud save with Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game saves a copy of your progress and purchase records to your Google account through Google Play Games, so you can restore them on your other Android devices. Settings shows your Google Play Games player name. This copy is stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can sign out, or delete your Play Games data, in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings.

Purchases

Google Play processes all payments. We never receive your card or payment details. The game receives product identifiers and purchase records from Google Play so it can verify purchases, deliver items and restore eligible purchases, such as Remove Ads. Google keeps its own payment records under its policies.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads). Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, your IP address and the approximate location it indicates, how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic information. It uses these to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads start, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. To learn more, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's advertising privacy information.

Watching a reward video is always optional. Buying Remove Ads stops the automatic ads between levels.

Things you choose to share

  • Backups: you can export a progress backup to a location you choose and import it later in Settings. Backups contain game progress but not purchase ownership.
  • Sharing: you can share a game result image or copy a diagnostic report using Android's sharing tools. You choose what to share and who receives it.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive whatever you send, such as your email address and message, and use it only to help you. We keep support emails for up to 24 months, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Reset Progress in Settings clears your puzzle progress. It keeps purchase records, so items you paid for aren't lost.
  • To remove all game data from your device, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • To delete cloud save data, use the Google Play Games app or your Google Account.
  • Exported backup files and any images or reports you shared stay wherever you put them. Delete those yourself.
  • For any privacy question or request, contact jr.dev.space@gmail.com.

Children

Rail Parade is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Information sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. We don't operate servers holding your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the updated version on this page with a new effective date.
